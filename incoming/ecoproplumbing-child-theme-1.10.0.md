Fixes from the website & search review of ecoproplumbing.ca (The Iconic Management, October 2026): the theme no longer contradicts itself, tells Google and AI assistants it is a plumber, and links every city page.
- **Fix**: One emergency response time everywhere. It was typed into three templates — 30 minutes on the home and service pages, 45 on the emergency page — and repeated as "30-minute response times" in the service-area copy. All of them now print `ecoproplumbing_response_minutes()`. Default **45** (the more cautious figure, confirmed by ticinoWEB), editable per site in ticinoWEB CMS → "Dati dell'azienda". An empty field hides every response-time claim and the "Lightning-Fast Response" card falls back to wording without a number.
- **Fix**: "Years in Business" on About us (was a hardcoded 15+) comes from the same section (`ecopro_years_in_business`, default 15).
- **Fix**: The business name is no longer typed into templates ("Eco Pro Plumbing", "Eco Pro Service Area", "Eco Pro Difference"); the site name from Settings → General is used, so there is one spelling to fix.
- **Fix**: Both new options survive a save of the parent's standalone Settings page, which posts the same option group without them (options.php would otherwise store null and hide the claims).
- **Feature (SEO)**: The parent's home-page Organization JSON-LD becomes a **Plumber** (LocalBusiness subtype) via `ticinoweb_seo_jsonld_org_schema`, with `@id`, phone, email and address from `ticinoweb_contact_*`, `openingHoursSpecification` from the CMS opening hours and `areaServed` from the service-area list. Fields left empty in the CMS are left out.
- **Feature (SEO)**: City pages get a `Service` JSON-LD for their town, provided by the same Plumber.
- **Feature**: City pages are linked from the **footer** ("Areas we serve", on every page), from the **front-page area tags** (a town with a page becomes a link) and from **every service page** and the emergency page ("<service> in your area"). New sidebar box "Pagina città" on pages says which town of the service-area list a page is about. Pages nobody assigned are matched by slug when exactly one top-level page ends in `-<town>`, so on ecoproplumbing.ca 11 of the 14 towns are linked without touching the database; Fergus, New Hamburg and St. Jacobs have no page yet.
- **Feature**: Overall Google rating, number of reviews and a "Read them all on Google" link above the four review cards. Same Places request as the reviews (`fields=reviews,rating,user_ratings_total,url`), cached 24 h; a failed call is cached for 1 h instead of being retried on every page view, and the request now has a timeout. Not added to the JSON-LD: Google does not accept third-party review ratings in a business's own markup.
- **Style**: Cookie banner in the site's colour, font and button radius (`--tw-cookie-primary`).
- **Not in the theme** (content of the site, to do in the editor): opening sentence of the city pages, "10+ years / 1000+ testimonials / 50+ technicians" in the Cambridge page text, the menu items "Sump Pump" → well pump and "Sewage Pump" → heating, pages for Fergus, New Hamburg and St. Jacobs, FAQ sheets for the city pages (the parent's "FAQ della pagina" box already prints them with FAQPage schema), price ranges, real job photos.
