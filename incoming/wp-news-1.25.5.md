
*   **Fixed**: a campaign could stay in *sending* forever when some recipients have an invalid email address. Those addresses were skipped in each batch but still counted as "pending", so the campaign was never completed and the recovery job re-launched it every few minutes, scanning the whole list each time (a campaign sent on 30 Sept 2026 kept a server busy for 34 days and flooded the PHP error log). Invalid addresses are now remembered per campaign, excluded from the pending count, logged once, and the campaign completes as *sent* when only they are left. No change for valid recipients, delivery errors or the retry logic.
