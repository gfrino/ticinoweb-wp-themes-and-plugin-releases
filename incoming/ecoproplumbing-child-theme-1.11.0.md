The owner does not promise minutes ("not realistic, dependent on geographic location or jobs booked that day"). A site with minutes set (ecoproplumbing.com) is unchanged; the area intro now says "we offer" instead of "we guarantee".
- **Change**: About us stat label "Years in Business" → "Years of Experience" (the owner's figure, 25+, is industry experience, not company age).
- **Feature**: `[eco_google_rating]` shortcode prints the live Google rating, number of reviews and link inside page text (aligned left). Replaces the "1000+ Customer Testimonials" claim on the Cambridge page with a figure that updates by itself. The home page uses the same function (`ecoproplumbing_reviews_summary()`), so the markup lives in one place.
